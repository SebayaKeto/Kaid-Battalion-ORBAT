## Notes

- **Start with section 1** (the weapons). They have no picture in the Codex at all right now, so they gain the most.
- If a weapon comes in more than one version in game (for example several DC-15 UGL variants), capture the one most troopers carry, or tell me and I'll add a button for each.
- **Leave out:** every "Authorized Kits" combination, for example a Medic carrying a Grenadier kit. That would multiply the list for little gain. Each billet shows its own kit, and the weapon kits cover the weapon options.
- **Skip anything that looks the same in game.** If two rows look identical, capture one and tell me which other row it covers. The site will reuse it.
